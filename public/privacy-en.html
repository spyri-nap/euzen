Local data and account

Exercises, workouts, plans, sessions, notes and settings are stored in the browser. Guest data remains separate from the signed-in account. Importing guest data requires explicit confirmation. Optional sign-in uses an email address and an emailed code. The configured Supabase test project handles sign-in, profiles and training synchronization. Pending account changes are synchronized when connected.

Device storage and backups

Browser cleanup and insufficient storage can remove local data. JSON backups contain training data and must be stored securely. Signing out or resetting locally does not delete the cloud account or other device copies. Export is available under Settings & Data. For access, correction and deletion, use the contact above; do not send access codes or backups.

Test access and feedback

Vercel hosts the test version and protects access through a separate sign-in. The beta guide stores its language selection locally. The feedback form processes entered text in the browser and can open an email draft. Only you send it through your email application. Training backups are not attached automatically. Do not enter codes, tokens or private training content.

Purpose and retention

Processing serves operation of the closed test, error analysis and app improvement. Test data, account data and voluntary feedback are retained until the operator decides on a specific deletion or restriction rule. Options under review are deletion after evaluation, a fixed test period followed by deletion, or regular review and deletion of unnecessary data. Request deletion at any time by email using the contact above. Include the email used for testing, but no codes or training backups.

Voluntary participation and health notice

Participation and feedback are voluntary. Eúzēn is a training and documentation tool and does not replace medical advice, examination or treatment. Consult qualified medical professionals for symptoms or questions about suitability for exercise.

Status of this information

This page describes implemented data behavior. Contractual privacy information concerning technical services and the legal basis for external testing have not been conclusively reviewed. This is not a fully reviewed privacy notice. Use only fictitious test content until these matters are clarified.

Health data and rights

Do not enter diagnoses, injuries, medication, pain, therapies or other health information. Processing special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR is not approved for this test phase. You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, object and withdraw consent. The appropriate legal basis and final retention period remain subject to legal review.
