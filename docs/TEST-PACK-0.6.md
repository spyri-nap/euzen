## English invitation draft

Subject: Your invitation to try Eúzēn

Hi,

I'd like to invite you to a small, closed test of Eúzēn — a space to create your own functional workouts and build a sustainable movement routine.

Use the versioned preview linked in RELEASE-0.6-TEST.md. You may first see Vercel's protected-access sign-in. Access must be approved separately; having an Eúzēn account alone does not grant Vercel access. If access is denied, contact me rather than creating extra accounts.

You can explore as a guest. For optional account synchronization, open Settings & Data, read the privacy information, confirm participation and sign in using the code sent to your email. Enter that code only in Eúzēn. Choose German or English in settings; the beta guide also supports Greek.

Please allow 15–20 minutes to create two fictional exercises, build a short workout, change its order, try a counter or timer, pause/resume, finish and review the result. Reopen the app and check whether your changes remain. This is a browser app with optional Home Screen installation, not an App Store download.

For this preparation phase use fictitious test content only; do not enter diagnoses, injuries, medication or real health/training histories. Participation and feedback are voluntary. Tell me what felt confusing or failed, using the short feedback questions below. Do not send codes, tokens or backups.

Support and feedback: info@nap-studios.de.

Thank you for helping shape Eúzēn!
Spyridon

## Shared installation and data notes

DE: Safari → Teilen → Zum Home-Bildschirm; Android Chrome → Installieren/Zum Startbildschirm. Gastdaten bleiben lokal und getrennt vom Konto. Lokal gespeichert bedeutet nicht synchronisiert. Erst nach bestätigter Offline-Vorbereitung offline testen. Vor Updates sichern, Training beenden und alle alten Fenster schließen. Einschränkungen und Prüfbefehle stehen in RELEASE-0.6-TEST.md.

EN: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; Android Chrome → Install/Add to Home Screen. Guest data stays local and separate from accounts. Saved locally does not mean synced. Test offline only after offline readiness is confirmed. Before updates, back up, finish training and close all old windows. See RELEASE-0.6-TEST.md for limitations and build commands.

EL: Safari → Κοινή χρήση → Προσθήκη στην οθόνη αφετηρίας· Android Chrome → Εγκατάσταση/Προσθήκη στην αρχική οθόνη. Τα δεδομένα επισκέπτη παραμένουν τοπικά και χωριστά από τον λογαριασμό. Τοπική αποθήκευση δεν σημαίνει συγχρονισμό. Δοκιμάστε χωρίς σύνδεση μόνο μετά την επιβεβαίωση ετοιμότητας. Πριν από ενημέρωση, εξάγετε αντίγραφο, ολοκληρώστε την προπόνηση και κλείστε όλα τα παλιά παράθυρα. Περιορισμοί και εντολές στο RELEASE-0.6-TEST.md.
